# Watch is a tab on the phone (2026-11-15, phone-watch v1.0.0 rule 1, part 3)

- The bar is Today · Watch · Chat while this phone has an Apple Watch paired, Isoform installed on
  it or not; with no watch it is Today · Chat and nothing about a watch shows. Built on the Today
  redesign (#1136), which removed Tasks.
- On Today, the mark at the top left opens the Watch tab (it blinks while the watch listens), and
  one amber line shows only when hours have waited for the computer 30 minutes or one could not
  be sent; it opens the Watch tab too.
- The "Your watch" card on Today is gone (it was the stand-in for the tab, #1086).
- Settings › Apple Watch opens the Watch tab (phone-settings v1.0.0 rule 7), shown only while a
  watch is paired. With no watch but recordings still held, Settings keeps a "Recordings" row to
  the list of hours, as before.
- The wide layout has no Watch tab yet: there the mark and the watch line open the Watch page as
  a sheet over Today.
