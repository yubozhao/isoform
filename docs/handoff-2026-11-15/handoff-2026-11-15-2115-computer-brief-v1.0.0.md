# Computer brief v1.0.0: the brief in full on the computer, Not right, and past briefs

Version 1.0.0 · written November 15, 2026, 9:15 PM Pacific, by Claude Design.

**Why this file:** the brief is rank 1, and computer-today v1.0.0 gave it a band with Read all ›, but nothing said what Read all opens on the computer. phone-brief v1.0.0 designed the brief in full for the phone; this is the same page at the computer's size, with the keyboard, plus Not right as a sheet and Past briefs.

**Board:** Isoform Computer, artboard 19 (The brief in full), frames 19a and 19b.

A change goes in a new file with a new version; this one isn't edited after it's written.

## Ranked goals

1. **It opens at once and plays,** from what this computer made.
2. **Its items are answered where they're mentioned,** with the keys.
3. **Any sentence can be corrected** in two keystrokes.

## 19a · The brief in full

1. **Read all opens it in Today's column,** with ‹ Today: the title (kind and time) and "{day} · made from today on this computer".
2. **The player:** play and pause (Space anywhere on the page), the bar with where it is and its length, 15 seconds back and on (← and →), the speed.
3. **Read-along:** while it plays, the sentence being read is highlighted and kept in view; scrolling away stops the follow until play is pressed again (phone-brief rule 3).
4. **Items sit where the brief mentions them,** as compact items answered with the keys (Y, N, M; Useful and Not useful for a result), focus moving with ↑ ↓; an answered one is one line with Undo (phone-brief rule 5). Agents' items follow brief-agents v1.0.0.
5. **Under any paragraph,** on hover or focus: **Reply about this** (Chat, with the sentence attached) and **Not right** (19b).
6. **On the right:** **In this brief**, its sources by kind (Calendar, Mail, Your watch, Your agents, Screen), each opening its Why; and **Past briefs** (rule 9).

## 19b · Not right

7. **Not right opens a small sheet:** "Not right?", the sentence quoted, "What's wrong with it? Optional." with the phone's reasons as chips (Didn't happen, Wrong person, Already done, Not mine), "Or say what's right", and "Tomorrow's brief won't say it, and what was made from it is marked for you to check." with **Cancel** and **Not right ⌘↩**.
8. **It's a No on that sentence,** kept in Your answers with its reason or the words given (redesign v1.0.0 rule 28); what was made from it shows "From a sentence you said wasn't right" in Your work for you to keep or drop.

## Past briefs

9. **By day, newest first:** each brief's kind and time, and where you are with it ("1 left to answer", "all answered", "not played"); a filled dot for not played, an open ring once played (phone-brief rules 10 and 11). "Every brief stays on this computer; the phone keeps 30 days." Friday evening's is "Your week".
