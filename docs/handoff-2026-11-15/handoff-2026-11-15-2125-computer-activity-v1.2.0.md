# Computer activity v1.2.0: Activity › Agents, and Settings › Your agents beside Agent of You

Version 1.2.0 · written November 15, 2026, 9:25 PM Pacific, by Claude Design.

**This file adds to computer-activity v1.0.0 and v1.1.0 (the Agents tab) and to computer-settings v1.1.0 (the two sections, drawn); everything in them stands.** It draws two places that were only described.

**Board:** Isoform Computer, new artboards 22 (Settings: Your agents and Agent of You) and 23 (Activity › Agents).

A change goes in a new file with a new version; this one isn't edited after it's written.

## Ranked goals

1. **Your agents are a source like any other:** in the open, by time, with Forget.
2. **"Your agents" and "Agent of You" never read as one thing.**

## Activity › Agents (artboard 23)

1. **What the tab holds, newest first:** every hand-off (when you said yes, What went, where it started); what Isoform knows each agent did (read it, came back, couldn't start, no word), each with its time; requests between agents with your answer or "Waiting for your yes, until {time} tomorrow"; and what Isoform learned from an agent's own history, only where Learn from it is on, each saying why it's there ("read because Learn from it is on for Codex").
2. **A request waiting** has **Open**, which opens it on Today; every other row has **Forget**.
3. **Forget on a hand-off removes Isoform's record of it, not what the agent made,** and the confirmation says so: "Forget your record of "{title}"? What {agent} made stays with {agent}." (computer-activity v1.1.0 rule 3).
4. **Agent rows also sit in Your day,** with the purple-blue dot of Your agents; learned rows use the screen's colour, since they're what an agent did on this computer.

## Settings › Your agents and Agent of You (artboard 22)

5. **Your agents:** the lead line ("The agents that do your work: Claude, ChatGPT, Codex and others you connect."); **Your jobs** (For code, For everything else), each with Change; **Other agents**, each Connected or Not connected, where it runs, Learn from it where possible ("Learn from it: off", or "only on this computer" in grey), and **Disconnect** (connected) or **Remove** (not connected); Connect an agent; thinks.note at the foot.
6. **Agent of You, right after:** its lead line ("You, as an agent clients can hire at isoform.ai/agents/{name}."); with the flag off, one card, "Soon", what it will be, and "When it opens, its set-up and records are here: your work profile, your rate, and every job." With the flag on, the beta's owner page (agent-of-you-beta v2.1.11, words from v2.1.12).
