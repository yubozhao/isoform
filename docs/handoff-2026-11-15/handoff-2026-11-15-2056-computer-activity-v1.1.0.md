## Ranked goals

1. **Forget says exactly what goes,** where it lives, and that it can't come back.
2. **An empty list says why it's empty,** once.

## 15e · Forget

1. **One confirmation:** "Forget "{what}"?" / "{what it is, its source and time}, and what was made from it:" / a list of everything that goes, each with where it lives ("Its note and the one thing you said you'd do · Watch"; "Reply to Sam, drafted · Your work"; ""Sam owns pricing" · You · Who you know") / "Gone from this computer, your phone and your watch. It can't be undone." / **Cancel** and **Forget all {n}** (redesign v1.0.0 rule 26).
2. **The button counts what goes,** including the thing itself. With nothing made from it, it reads **Forget**.
3. **Work an agent already has** is listed as "Sent to {agent} · stays with {agent}", and isn't counted: Isoform can't take it back from there (agents-join rule 23).
4. **No Undo after Forget.** The row leaves at once; Activity says nothing about it afterwards.

## 15f · You with nothing new

5. **When every claim is judged:** "Nothing new to check this week." / "You've judged all {n} things Isoform thinks about you. New ones show here first as it sees more of your work." Then Confirmed, with how each was confirmed ("You confirmed · {date}", "You edited", "You said").
6. **Before Isoform has anything to say** (the first days): one line, "Isoform hasn't seen enough to say anything about you yet. Claims show here as it sees your work, usually after a day." No examples and no sample claims.
7. **Confirmed's count** uses agent-words v1.0.0: "{n} · Agent of You can say {m}".
