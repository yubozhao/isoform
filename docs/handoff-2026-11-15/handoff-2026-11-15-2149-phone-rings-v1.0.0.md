# Phone rings v1.0.0: notify v1.0.0 and computer-trouble v1.0.0, drawn on the phone

Version 1.0.0 · written November 15, 2026, 9:49 PM Pacific, by Claude Design.

**This file draws, on the phone, what notify v1.0.0 and computer-trouble v1.0.0 decided; their rules stand, and it adds the phone's words for Settings › Notifications.** It changes phone-settings v1.0.0 rule 8's Notifications page only where it says so.

**Board:** Isoform iPhone, new artboard 17 (Where it rings, and trouble, on the phone), frames 17a to 17d.

A change goes in a new file with a new version; this one isn't edited after it's written.

## The frames

1. **17a · Away, the phone rings.** With the computer locked, the brief rings on the phone at its time (notify v1.0.0 rules 1, 2 and B2).
2. **17b · Read anywhere, gone everywhere.** The brief read on the computer at 6:10 PM is gone from the lock screen by 6:12 PM (notify v1.0.0 rule 8, B7). The dashed box on the board is a note, not something the phone shows.
3. **17c · Settings › Notifications.** In order: the line on where Isoform rings; **Three kinds, and nothing else**, with one switch per kind and its one-line why; the Time Sensitive line under them; Quiet hours and the count on the app icon (as phone-settings v1.0.0); and the quiet-hours line under them.
4. **17d · Trouble on the phone.** One amber line under the date, no button (computer-trouble v1.0.0 rule 8); the brief still comes and ends with what it's missing.
