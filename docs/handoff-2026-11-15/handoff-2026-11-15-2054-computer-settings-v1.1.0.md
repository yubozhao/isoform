# Computer settings v1.1.0: Agent of You keeps its place in Settings, and what You's Agent of You tab shows

Version 1.1.0 · written November 15, 2026, 8:54 PM Pacific, by Claude Design.

**This file adds to computer-settings v1.0.0 and v1.0.1, and changes computer-you v1.0.0 rule 5; everything else in them stands.** computer-settings v1.0.0 listed Settings' sections without Agent of You. agent-of-you-beta v2.1.11 §1 keeps the owner's page in Settings › Agent of You "in every state", and the phone keeps it there too (phone-settings v1.0.0 rule 5). I left it out by mistake; this puts it back and says how it sits beside Your agents.

A change goes in a new file with a new version; this one isn't edited after it's written.

## Ranked goals

1. **Nothing designed and building is dropped** by a later pass.
2. **Agent of You and your agents never look like one thing** (agent-words v1.0.0).

## Settings

1. **Sections, in this order:** What Isoform sees, Closed Eyes, Your data, Your agents, **Agent of You**, Your phone, Your watch, Briefs and notifications, About. (Replaces computer-settings v1.0.0 rule 1's list.)
2. **Settings › Agent of You is agent-of-you-beta v2.1.11's owner page, unchanged:** set-up (the four steps), the work profile, rate, switches, and records. While the website's flag is off, the section shows "Agent of You" with "Soon" and nothing else, as on the phone (phone-settings v1.0.0 rule 5).
3. **Your agents comes first and Agent of You right after it,** each with its own one-line description at the top of its section, so the two are never read as one:
   - Your agents: "The agents that do your work: Claude, ChatGPT, Codex and others you connect."
   - Agent of You: "You, as an agent clients can hire at isoform.ai/agents/{name}."

## You's Agent of You tab

4. **computer-you v1.0.0 rule 5 changes:** the tab shows the claims Agent of You may say (redesign-you v1.0.0 rules 10 and 11, with agent-words v1.0.0's words: "Agent of You can say this"), each with Right? and the switch to stop it saying that one, and at the top "Set up and records are in Settings › Agent of You ›". While the flag is off, the tab says "Soon: Agent of You answers clients from what you've judged here." as before.
5. **Questions clients ask that Agent of You can't answer** are items in Your work, as redesign-you v1.0.0 rules 20 to 23 say; not in this tab.
